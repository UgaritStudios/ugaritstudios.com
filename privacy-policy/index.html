Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ugarit Studios ("Ugarit Studios", "we", "us") handles information in connection with our website at ugaritstudios.com (the "Site"), the content channels we operate, and our internal publishing tool, Ugarit Channels Publisher (the "API Client"). Ugarit Studios is an independent studio based in Ontario, Canada.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

Our API Client uses YouTube API Services. By using or authorizing the API Client, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information accessed through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

The API Client is an internal tool. It is used only by Ugarit Studios to publish and manage videos on YouTube channels that Ugarit Studios owns and operates. It is not offered, sold or distributed to the public or to third parties.

2. Information the API Client accesses

When a channel owned by Ugarit Studios is authorized through Google OAuth, the API Client accesses only the following, and only for that channel:

  • basic channel information (channel ID, title and handle), to confirm which channel it is publishing to;
  • the videos, captions and thumbnails we upload to that channel, and their metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, category, language, privacy status, scheduled publish time and content disclosures);
  • the processing and publishing status of those uploads.

The API Client does not access other users' channels, private messages, subscriber lists, comments, watch history or any personal data of YouTube viewers. It does not download or scrape YouTube content, and it does not automate likes, comments, subscriptions or any other engagement.

3. How we use, store and share that information

  • Use: solely to upload our own videos and set their metadata, thumbnails and captions, and to confirm they were processed correctly.
  • Storage: OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted in our workflow automation platform (n8n Cloud). Video IDs, titles and publishing dates are kept in our internal production records (Google Workspace) so we can track what we published.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, rent or share data obtained through YouTube API Services with any third party. Data is processed only by the service providers that run our tools (Google, n8n) under their own terms, and only to operate the API Client.
  • Retention: we keep publishing records for as long as the related video remains on our channel, and delete stored tokens within 30 days after a channel's authorization is revoked or the channel is removed from the API Client.

4. Revoking access and deleting data

Access granted to the API Client can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, the API Client can no longer reach the channel, and we delete the stored tokens and any data obtained through YouTube API Services for that channel within 30 days. You can also request deletion at any time by emailing us at the address below; we will confirm deletion within 30 days.

5. Information collected by this website

The Site is a simple informational website. It has no account sign-up and does not ask visitors for personal information. Our hosting provider, Squarespace, may automatically collect standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) and may place cookies or similar technologies on your device to operate and secure the Site and to provide basic traffic statistics. See Squarespace's Privacy Policy for details. You can control cookies through your browser settings. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

6. Children

The Site and the API Client are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Material changes will be posted on this page.
