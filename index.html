Our name

Why Ugarit?

Ugarit was a Bronze Age kingdom near modern Latakia, Syria, a crossroads of trade, languages and ideas. Its clay tablets preserved poems, letters and one of the earliest alphabets. We named our studio after it because good stories, like good alphabets, are meant to travel.
